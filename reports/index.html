What the replicators found (spoiler: read after looking at the reports)
  • A replication report from the Institute for Replication (Auspurg & Brüderl, 2026, I4R DP 303) points to the four single-team discipline codes: they absorb 80 estimates from four teams that go against the hypothesis.
  • Merge those four codes, and the per-point ideology effect falls from 0.011 (p = .06) to 0.001 (p = .94). The pro–anti contrast that all four agents chose as the main result falls from 0.085 (p = .008) to 0.044 (p = .23) (my calculation).
  • Dropping the regression weights, or using the original study's standardised estimates, also removes the per-point effect.
  • The authors reply that their discipline coding is a theory-driven choice (Borjas & Breznau, 2026, I4R DP 304).

Two agents saw the single-team codes. None tested whether they change the result.
